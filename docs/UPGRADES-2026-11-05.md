# Performance, tactical AI and target previews

Implemented on `fix/critical-evaluation`, based on `d86bb9d`. This batch implements the four priorities in the latest QA report.

## Changes

1. Cached, pooled sprites replace repeated vector rebuilding for ground stains, chimney smoke and rain. The stain pool remains bounded at 420 and expires/reuses its sprites. The game simulation and seeded randomness are unchanged by these rendering changes.
2. Pacifist Love, Whisper faction ties and the legacy guess policy use seeded tie breaks. An opt-in decision/position trace identifies actual travel problems. Walking goals now stay inside the safe interior as the storm closes, and villagers abandon an armory that is no longer reachable. Arm previews and private submission feedback explain the closure.
3. Bots can retreat wounded survivors to safer places, escort them with nearby healthy villagers, guard after losses, and prioritize emergency healing/shielding. Ordinary orders exclude villagers already assigned to a rescue. Duplicate guard orders merge without wasting an order slot. Defensive planning uses a copied public-information view, without enemy orders or hidden temperaments/opinions. Existing difficulty resource and hesitation rules still apply.
4. Hover, keyboard focus or touch hold previews quick actions. Staged order labels also preview their plan; the order builder previews a complete draft. Gold outlines show your villagers and blue marks the target or place. Random selections show the eligible pool and an upper limit, without rolling RNG. Touch holds do not stage an order. Escape, submission, phase changes and hotseat handoffs clear the preview. The existing compact action buttons and four-card grid remain.

## Verification

`npm run check` passes all ten groups: 30 regression tests, simulation smoke, parser, orders, verbs, powers, effects, six delayed-network matches, 12 fuzz matches and production build.

The four newly failing legacy effect comparisons were investigated before acceptance. Avoid/Hunt comparisons had active opposing bot orders/miracles; Aegis used an already injured victim and Sleep could be interrupted by bot powers. Their single-effect fixtures now hold opponents still, use independent seat objects and retain their original effect thresholds. Full bot matches, fuzzing and multiplayer still run separately. The isolated results were Avoid 159.8 versus 85.1 distance, Hunt 100 versus 0 damage, Aegis 15 versus 38 damage, and Sleep stationary for the first six seconds and awake after eleven.

Local Chrome tests passed:

- Six viewport compact-action/card checks and 42 scroll checks across three sizes.
- Hover/focus and emulated touch-hold previews, read-only state/RNG, three staged orders contained, hotseat privacy and effect-pool capacity/reuse.
- Four-step illustrated guide, news settings, draft/timeout submission, roster, settings, day-3/day-4 locks, day-5 area unlocks and final-faction celebration.
- Easy, normal and hard solo journeys to days 10, 16 and 10; four-player hotseat to day 8. These use UI submissions and accelerated rendered frame calls; solo can skip the remaining bot match after the human faction falls.
- Four isolated browser contexts over real PeerJS/WebRTC to day 20, with 131 actions and 61 transitions. All four final simulation states and RNG streams matched. No page errors or desyncs. Lobby joining needed two retries.

## Matched render measurement

Same day-6 baseline simulation, 31 survivors, 420 stains, 1440x900 Chrome. ABBA order, 120 warmup frames and 600 measured frames in each run. Before/after simulation hashes matched at both boundaries.

| Renderer | Mean update/render work per frame | Measured FPS | p95 frame interval |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Before, two runs | 11.41 ms | 59.7-59.8 | 16.8 ms |
| After, two runs | 6.56 ms | 59.9-60.0 | 16.8 ms |

Measured work fell 42.5%. This is main-thread elapsed time inside the update/render call, not a GPU utilization measurement. An earlier cold baseline reached only 41 FPS, but its warm repeat reached 60. The supported conclusion is lower frame work and more headroom, not a universal 46% FPS gain.

## AI trial, 45-second days

160 matches against the previous build with the same seeds, four seat rotations and personality lineups. Shared seeds/rotations are correlated; these are descriptive wins per appearance, not human balance certification.

| Personality | Before | After |
|---|---:|---:|
| Warmonger | 32.0% | 33.6% |
| Pacifist | 23.5% | 17.6% |
| Opportunist | 42.2% | 35.9% |
| Gossip | 19.0% | 25.9% |
| Chaos | 8.3% | 12.9% |

Mean length changed from 12.36 to 11.78 turns. After: 150 last-faction victories and 10 score decisions at the safety limit. Seat wins were 43/39/40/38. This is not evidence that all personalities are equally strong; pacifist performance needs further human playtesting and broader balance work. No faction stat buffs were added to force equal wins.

Twelve position-traced matches recorded zero stationary villagers away from their destination after the movement fix. Short state reversals still occur during ordinary social behavior and danger response. No blanket decision cooldown was added.

## Reproduce and remaining limits

Run `npm ci`, `npm run check`, and `node tools/ai-trace.js 12 17000`. Detailed local browser drivers, screenshots and raw results are in the sibling `Tiny-Gods-Upgrade-Verification` directory. Its report explains the baseline server needed for the rendering comparison.

Physical phone controls, listening to audio, assistive technology, long sessions, restrictive networks, cross-device play and human strategy/fun remain owner playtests. Automated checks cannot guarantee an error-free game. Original main is preserved; this work is published only to the fix branch.
