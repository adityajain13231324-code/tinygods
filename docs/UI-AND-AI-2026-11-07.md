# Menu, onboarding and combat clarity

Implemented on fix/critical-evaluation.

- Solo opens faction, difficulty, planning time, day length and news setup. Selected faction changes the human seat; the remaining factions are bots.
- First menu visit opens a skippable guide. Skip, Ready, Escape and outside dismissal record completion. Invite links resume opening their lobby after the guide.
- How to Play includes a four-influence allocation demonstration with twelve workers, editable split and an animated outcome example. Detailed rules remain available below.
- Home and dialogs use consistent cards, spacing, keyboard focus and responsive layouts. Online entry separates hosting from joining.
- Peaceful speech fades near combat; background chatter is suppressed locally. Own-faction and combat status thoughts retain emphasis. Launcher holders display five ammunition dots.
- Combat target preference is stronger, optional target changes have a one-second commitment, guard repositioning has wider spacing, and chats validate reciprocal participants. Immediate danger, explicit orders, blackout and target death retain priority.

Validation: full verification suite, browser input regressions, and desktop/mobile onboarding/setup/faction/online validation. Screenshots are in test-results. The broad AI diagnostic still exceeds its reversal target: this is a bounded improvement, not a claim that every reversal is erroneous or eliminated. Multiplayer connections across different physical networks and audio listening were not repeated.

Run menu checks against localhost:4181 with node tools/menu-review.mjs. As with browser-inputs.mjs, Playwright and a browser are required; BROWSER_EXECUTABLE can select an installed browser.

Final 12-match AI sample after preventing optional arguments from interrupting assigned work: reversals fell from 2.82 to 1.91 per villager-minute (about 32%), voluntary fed-up retarget events fell from 2065 to 1394, and no villagers were flagged as stuck. These are bounded bot samples, not proof of all human gameplay outcomes. The diagnostic target below 0.5 remains unmet.
